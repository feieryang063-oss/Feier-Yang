Hydro Planter

A planter that waters plants directly at the root without touching or damaging the plant, designed for rotomolding production; doubles as an outdoor stool when empty.

CategoryHome Goods
Year2018
CreditsFeier Yang

About

The hydro planter waters plants directly to their roots without touching or damaging the plant. It is designed for a plastic factory under the production method of rotomolding. The planter can be used as an outdoor stool when there is no plant in the pot.

How it works

Water goes in from the tube and directly moistens the roots. Extra water leaks out from the other tube that faces towards the ground.
