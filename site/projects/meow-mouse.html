Product Design

Meow Mouse

A computer mouse designed wide and round for ergonomic comfort, with a sink-in side profile that lets the thumb rest naturally.

CategoryDigital Devices
Year2019
CreditsFeier Yang

About

A mouse designed to be wide and round so that it can be ergonomically more comfortable to hold. The sink-in on the side of the mouse helps users naturally rest the thumb while holding the mouse. Shown are examples for right-handed users; left-handed users would use a mirrored version.
