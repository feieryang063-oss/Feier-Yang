About

Hi, I'm Feier (Claire) Yang

I'm a product manager based in Chicago, currently building restaurant software. I care about the craft of making things simple, useful and a little bit delightful — whether that means running a sprint, sketching a toy concept, or shaping the UX of a smart device.

My background runs from hardware to SaaS. Over the past few years I've worked across product design, UX/UI and project management — shipping healthcare devices, children's products, home goods and speculative concepts from research through to final prototype.
